01 / ABOUT ME

A little context.
A lot of curiosity.

I’m Rickie (Ruike) Lin, a master’s student in Industrial Engineering at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. I’m interested in how people interact with emerging technologies—and how we can design those technologies around human needs.

My background spans Information Science and Data Science. Through user research, statistical analysis, and interface design, I explore the connection between what the data reveals and what people experience. Away from my studies, you’ll often find me outdoors with my two dogs.
